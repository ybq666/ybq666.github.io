About Me

I am currently a graduate student at the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Nanjing University (NJU). My academic journey is driven by deep curiosity about artificial intelligence, foundation models, and software intelligence.

My primary research interests include Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Software Engineering / Code Intelligence. I am dedicated to exploring model reasoning capabilities, efficient inference, and intelligent developer tools.
