About Me 个人简介

I am currently a graduate student at the Software Institute, Nanjing University (NJU). My academic journey is driven by deep curiosity about artificial intelligence, foundation models, and software engineering.

My primary research interests include Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Software Engineering / Code Intelligence. I am dedicated to exploring model reasoning capabilities, code intelligence, and intelligent developer systems.

我是南京大学软件学院的在读研究生。我的学术兴趣聚焦于人工智能基础模型与前沿软件智能技术。

主要研究方向涵盖大语言模型（LLM）、自然语言处理（NLP）以及代码智能与软件工程。致力于探索更稳健的模型推理机制、代码生成与智能化开发者协同系统。
